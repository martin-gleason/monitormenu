## What this means for SwitchBar

- **Input switching from a menu bar app with a CLI and hotkeys already exists** (OpenDisplay, Lunar, m1ddc + Raycast). SwitchBar is not first there.
- **Nobody ships PiP/PbP in a menu bar app.** m1ddc has it on the command line, for Dell, with hard-coded values. Dell's own app skips the G3223D. That is SwitchBar's uncovered ground (FR4).
- **Nobody states a whitelist rule.** Every tool either hard-codes VCP values or lets the user send anything. D3 (offer and write only what the capabilities string advertises) is a differentiator and a safety property.
- **m1ddc's documented Dell values seed the PiP labels.** The G3223D advertises `E9(00 01 02 21 22 24 29 2A 2D 2E)`; 0x00, 0x21, 0x22 and 0x24 are now labelled from a documented source. 0x01, 0x02, 0x29, 0x2A, 0x2D and 0x2E still need the mapping session (`O5`), shown as hex until then (D3).
- **The `IOAVService` path is well trodden** (m1ddc, Didact, FreeDisplay, MonitorControl). D1 carries less risk than RR1's wording suggests; the risk is Apple changing it, not it being unexplored.
- **Scope discipline matters.** OpenDisplay shows what happens without it: fourteen feature areas pre-1.0. SwitchBar's out-of-scope list in the spec is the defence.
