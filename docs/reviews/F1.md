## What the fixes surfaced

Re-running the URL-scheme PiP cycle after the fixes exposed a behaviour the first build had masked: **every PiP (and presumably input) change makes the G3223D retrain its link a few seconds later.** The display leaves CoreGraphics (id becomes unknown), sometimes leaves the IORegistry for several seconds, and the `IOAVService` handle dies with `MACH_SEND_INVALID_DEST`. Fixes `7a…`–`HEAD`: fail fast on that code, rediscover and retry up to three rounds a second apart, key everything by serial, wait up to eight seconds for monitors when an action finds the list empty, and pre-schedule a rediscovery after input and PiP actions. Evidence: two full on/off cycles at 20:23–20:24, every action logged as succeeded (each after one retry round), final state read back Off, brightness 56, input HDMI 1. The CoreGraphics reconfiguration callback does fire, but up to ten seconds after the write, so it cannot be the only trigger. RR9 updated.
